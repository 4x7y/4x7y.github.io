  • Finding a feasible grasp
  • Solving the inverse kinematics

Settings:

  • No explicit target configuration

Methodology:

  • Combines the search for a collision-free motion with the online search for a feasible grasp.
  • Hand = grasping center point (GCP) + approach direction (mandually defined).
