Şucan, Ioan A., and Lydia E. Kavraki. “Kinodynamic motion planning by interior-exterior cell exploration.“ Algorithmic Foundation of Robotics VIII. Springer, Berlin, Heidelberg, 2009. 449-464.

For system with complex dynamics:

  • Physics based simulation is necessary
  • High dimensionality
  • Sampling valid state is difficult

Strategies:

  • Coverage estimation help the planner detect the less explored areas of the state space.
  • Bias on the boundary of the explored region.

Formal Definition:

  • A motion $\mu = (s, u, t) = (\text{start state}, \text{control input}, \text{duration})$
  • A grid $G = \lbrace L_1, L_2, L_3 \rbrace$ is a multi-level discretization of state space
  • A cell chain $\boldsymbol{c} = (p_1, p_2, p_3)$ where $p_i \in L_i$ and $p_i$ is inside $p_{i+1}$

Algorithm

  1. Let $\mu_0$ be the motion of duration $0$ containing solely $q_{\rm start}$
  2. Create an empty Grid data-structure $G$
  3. $G$.AddMotion($\mu_0$)
  4. for $i \leftarrow 1\cdots N$ iterations do
  5. $\quad$ Select a cell chain $\boldsymbol{c}$ from $G$, with a bias on exterior cells (70%)
  6. $\quad$ Select $\mu$ from $\boldsymbol{c}$ according to a half normal distribution
  7. $\quad$ Select $s$ along $\mu$
  8. $\quad$ Sample random control $u \in U$ and simulation time $t \in \mathbb{R}^+$
  9. $\quad$ Check if any motion $(s, u, t_o)$, $t_o \in (0, t]$ is valid (forward propagation)
  10. $\quad$ if a motion is found then
  11. $\qquad$ Construct the valid motion $\mu_o = (s, u, t_o)$ with $t_o$ maximal
  12. $\qquad$ if $\mu_o$ reaches the goal region, return path to $\mu_o$
  13. $\qquad$ $G$.AddMotion($\mu_o$)
  14. $\quad$ end if
  15. $\quad$ for every level $L_j$ do
  16. $\qquad$ $P_j = \alpha + \beta$. (ratio of increase in coverage of $L_j$ to simulated time)
  17. $\qquad$ Multiply the score of cell $p_j$ in $\boldsymbol{c}$ by $P_j$ iff $P_j < 1$
  18. $\quad$ end for
  19. end for

Remarks:

  1. Cell chain $\boldsymbol{c}$ is selected based on importance score:where
    • $\mathcal{I}$ stands for the number of the iteration at which $p$ was created,
    • $K$ is initialized to $1$ but may later be updated when expanding from $p$,
    • $\mathcal{S}$ is the number of times $p$ was selected for expansion,
    • $\mathcal{N}$ is the number of instantiated neighboring cells at the same level of discretization, and
    • $\mathcal{C}$ is a positive measure of coverage for $p$
      • For $L_1$, $\mathcal{C} = \sum_{\mu \in p} \mu.t$
      • For $L_{2}, L_3$, $\mathcal{C} = \text{number of initiated child cells of } p$
  2. Motion $\mu$ is selected according to a half-normal distribution, such that motions added more recently are preferred for expansion.
  3. A state $s$ along $\mu$ is then chosen uniformly at random.
  4. Expanding the tree of motions continues from $s$.
  5. Exterior cells:
    Exterior cells has less than $2n$ instantiated neighboring cells (diagonal neighboring cells are ignored) at the same level of discretization.
