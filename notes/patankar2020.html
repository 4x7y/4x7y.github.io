Patankar, Aditya, Amin Fakhari, and Nilanjan Chakraborty. “Hand-Object Contact Force Synthesis for Manipulating Objects by Exploiting Environment.”

Problem:

  • Motors are not powerful enough to balance the weight of the object.
  • Find the optimal contact forces by exploiting object-environment contacts.

Assumptions:

  • Soft-finger contacts
  • Quasi-static manipulation

Constraints:

  • Friction cone constraints (formulated as Second order cone) at manipulator and environment contacts.
  • Upper bounds for the normal contact forces.
  • Force equilibrium (Quasi-static assumption).

Objective:

  • Grasping force optimization problem (GFOP)
    • $\min \max_i (F_{n, i})$
    • $\min \max_i (|F_{n, i}|)$
  • Torque optimization problem (TOP) $$
    • $\min \max_i (\tau_i)$

Formulated as a second order cone problem (SOCP), which is a convex optimization problem and can be solved by CVX toolbox.

Future works:

  • Trajectory planning for heavy object pivoting.
