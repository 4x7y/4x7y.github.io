“PnuGrip: An Active Two-Phase Gripper for Dexterous Manipulation”

https://youtu.be/dtDefve8KFk

The design of a two-phase parallel jaw gripper with an active pneumatic brake is presented in this paper. The finger enables re-orientation of a grasped object by using a pneumatic braking mechanism to transition between free-rotating and fixed (i.e., braked) phases. Several brake configurations and mechanisms are analyzed and a pneumatic disk-brake is selected since it both satisfies the design requirements and relies on a readily available power source. The gripper has several advantages over previous designs. The most important is the ability to transition between the free and brake phases independent of the grasping force. Other advantages include a compact and simple design to enable grasping in clutter, an encoder to estimate the rotation of the grasped object, and quick and repeatable actuation.
