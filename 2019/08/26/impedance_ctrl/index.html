Impedance Control

Yuechuan's Blog
2019-08-26
control
  • Impedance control is suited for tasks in which contact forces should be “kept small”, while their accurate regulation is not mandatory.
  • The desired performance is specified through a generalized dynamic impedance, namely a complete set of mass-spring-damper equations (typically chosen as linear and decoupled, but also nonlinear).
  • Since a control loop based on force error is missing, forces are only indirectly assigned by controlling position.

Choice of Impedance Model

  • Low contact forces

    Large$M_{m, i}$ and small $K_{m,i}$ in Cartesian directions where contact is foreseen

  • Good tracking

    Large $K_{m, i}$ and small $M_{m, i}$ in Cartesian directions that are supposed to be free
